An exploration prototype, not the finished game. A fixed test town, local NPC dialogue, simplified first-person rendering. No account or connection required.

Controls & installation

Phone: landscape recommended. Left stick moves; drag the scene to look. Tap people to talk. Triple tap objects to inspect. Hold DUCK to watch a location. PC: WASD, drag to look, E interact, F inspect, Space jump, C crouch, M map, N notes. Escape closes panels.

On iPhone, open the hosted game in Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen. Offline play becomes available after one successful online load. Export important saves from Map → Saves.
